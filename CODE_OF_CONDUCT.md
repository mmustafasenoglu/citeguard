# Code of Conduct

We want citeguard to be a productive, technically rigorous, and respectful open-source project.

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss technical disagreements without personal attacks;
- provide reproducible evidence for bugs and behavioral claims;
- respect privacy and intellectual-property boundaries when sharing academic text;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or abusive behavior;
- follow maintainer guidance on repository scope and review standards.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
